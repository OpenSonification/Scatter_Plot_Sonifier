Variability adjustment

0% · baseline

Press V to enable variance adjustment, then press plus or equals to increase it and minus or underscore to decrease it. Direct plus or minus presses do nothing until N, V, or B has selected an adjustment mode. The buttons provide the same variance actions. Every newly loaded example resets this adjustment to 0%.

Positive values increase variance toward the edges; negative values concentrate it near the centre.

The model uses normalized x values from 0 to 1. With quadratic mean mode enabled, raw y = q(x − 0.50)² + mx + b + ε. Gaussian noise uses ε = σ(x)z, where z ~ N(0,1), and log Var(ε | x) = log(σ₀²) + h₁(x − 0.50) + h₂(x − 0.50)². The defaults h₁ = h₂ = 0 give constant variance. If the curve or any generated point leaves y = 0–1, the complete raw y extent is automatically rescaled into the selected frequency range instead of being clipped. The solid curve is a boundary-corrected local-quadratic Gaussian smoother of the displayed points. At the left and right extremes it uses a one-sided fit, avoiding the inward bias caused by truncated edge bins.

Keyboard and screen-reader help

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move through native buttons, sliders, checkboxes, menus, and links. Arrow keys change a focused slider by one step; Page Up and Page Down make a larger change; Home and End move to its limits. A focused checkbox can be toggled with either Space or Enter.

  • Alt+1 through Alt+9, plus Alt+0, move focus directly to the ten destinations listed in the Keyboard navigation menu above.
  • Alt+# toggles “Read focused items aloud with a browser voice” without deliberately moving keyboard focus. This shortcut remains available even when single-key commands are disabled.
  • With single-key shortcuts enabled, 1 through 9 load the matching gallery example without starting audio. Only the selected example name is announced. Press P for Points (backtick and F1 remain alternatives), O to stop Points, S for Smooth, or A to stop Smooth.
  • Press N to enable point-count adjustment. Then plus or equals steps upward and minus or underscore steps downward through 100, 200, 500, 1,000, 2,000, 5,000, and 10,000 points. Every newly loaded example resets to exactly 1,000 points.
  • Press V to enable variance adjustment. Then plus or equals increases the current variance adjustment by 20 percentage points, while minus or underscore decreases it. Loading another example resets it to baseline.
  • Press B to enable stereo-balance adjustment. Then plus or equals steps upward and minus or underscore steps downward through 0%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80%, and 100%.
  • Only the currently enabled N, V, or B mode responds to plus and minus. The two-word mode message is shown and announced. Press R to generate a fresh random sample.
  • In the plot explorer, Left and Right Arrow move one position, Home and End jump to the extremes, and Enter previews the current tone.
  • Press Enter while the canvas has focus to move directly to the plot explorer. Escape returns focus to the left control rail without stopping playback.
  • Single-key commands are enabled initially and can be turned off under Accessibility preferences if they conflict with screen-reader browse commands or speech input.

Version 2D places examples and navigation in a left rail, with playback, the plot, optional keyboard exploration, and data alternatives in the right workspace. Gallery selections retain a persistent pressed state. N, V, and B select point-count, variance, and balance adjustment modes for subsequent plus and minus presses. On narrow screens, the workspace appears before the detailed settings. The file remains self-contained and does not upload data.
